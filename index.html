ABOUT WEEKEND WATCHER

For the fan who stays curious.

This is for people who enjoy sport, including the games they don’t yet know to look for. Choose any dates from today through 28 days ahead, then explore the major occasions and the events you might otherwise miss. Narrow by sport, day, viewing hours or verified story tags.

Your curiosity sets the range. Preferences stay in your browser, without an account.

Your filters

League and story filters narrow your picks. Select multiple tags to match any of them, or require all. The main recommendations are already global. League and story filters narrow that same worldwide slate; the optional outside-hours shelf is clearly separate.

All includes open competitions. A women’s bout in an open competition can appear under Women’s. Country relevance is inferred from the selected time zone; it is not exact geolocation and only provides a small ranking nudge.

Time & ranking

Best overall ranks the biggest qualifying events worldwide, including U.S. sports. Discover something switches U.S.-centered sports off by default and then uses the same occasion-first ranking. You can override the U.S. sports toggle at any time.

Ranked matchup is primarily a college-football signal: both NCAA FBS teams must be in the active top 25. Weekend Watcher uses CFP rankings once available and AP Top 25 beforehand. If the upcoming schedule week has no poll snapshot yet, it falls back to the latest prior valid poll. A single ranked team can still show its rank without creating the tag.

Preset hours use your selected time zone. A custom overnight window belongs to the day it starts. Equal custom start/end means all day. Long multi-day events without verified playing sessions are withheld from this pilot. Duration estimates can change. Best Overall is occasion-first: championships, elimination games, playoffs and elite ranked matchups get hard ranking floors. Time fit and country relevance are bounded nudges after the hard availability filters. Preseason, friendlies and exhibitions are excluded from the live product. Discover Something uses the same base score, then adds a modest variety bonus. Team-strength enrichment is still incomplete.

If necessary games are hidden by default because they may never be played. Turn them on to see them; they carry a visible If necessary tag and receive no ranking penalty. Avoid overlaps chooses the longest fitting session for each event, then maximizes the current fit score across a non-overlapping set.

What is connected

The local server uses your TheSportsDB subscription to import the global schedule. An optional CollegeFootballData connection adds NCAA FBS games and AP/CFP ranking evidence. MLB’s official schedule adds postseason round context and probable pitchers when supplied. Official KBO and NPB schedules also verify baseball fixtures and add playoff dates; unpublished times stay pending. Both private keys stay server-side. Unknown tags and missing stats are not invented. There are no runtime AI calls.

Weekend Watcher deliberately stops at discovery. There is no channel or streaming guide; once you find the event, use your preferred search or sports service. Fixtures and times can change, and coverage is not guaranteed complete.
