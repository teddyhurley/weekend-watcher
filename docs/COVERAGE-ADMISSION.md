# Coverage Admission

Weekend Watcher no longer treats 500K average viewership as a hard universal cutoff. It remains one objective admission path, but global discovery needs to cover important competitions whose audience measurement is fragmented or not directly comparable across countries.

A competition can be admitted through:

- **Audience:** sourced average live audience >= 500,000 in a meaningful market.
- **Top tier:** major top-flight domestic competition; England’s Championship is the sole standing second-tier exception.
- **Major cup / continental competition:** nationally or continentally significant knockout competition.
- **Global event:** major senior international championship or major global tour.

Admission never adds points to an event’s Watch Score. Event importance is evaluated separately.

Hard exclusions: exhibitions/friendlies, preseason, esports, horse racing and scripted sports entertainment.
